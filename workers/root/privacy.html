Privacy on raveh.dev

raveh.dev hosts separate websites on its subdomains. For information about the website you were using, including its data practices, your rights, and how to contact its operator, refer to that website's Privacy Policy.

The following applies to any website on raveh.dev that displays ads.

Advertising

We request non-personalized ads only. Google AdSense selects these ads using context such as the page's content and your general location, rather than your browsing history.

Google and the advertising partners identified in the consent message may receive your IP address, the page URL, device information, and ad interactions. They may use cookies, local storage, and identifiers to limit repeated ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Non-personalized advertising does not mean anonymous or cookie-free advertising.

See how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's Privacy Policy for its data practices, retention, and international processing. Other advertising partners and their privacy policies are available through the consent message.

Your choices

We use Google's consent and privacy messages where applicable. Where consent is required, ads wait for your permission. If you refuse, withdraw consent, or opt out of advertising data sharing, we do not show ads. We also honor Global Privacy Control opt-out signals.

Use the website's Privacy choices control to review or change your decision. You can also manage Google's advertising preferences in Ad Settings and delete or block cookies through your browser settings.
